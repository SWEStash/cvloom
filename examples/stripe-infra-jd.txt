Senior Infrastructure Engineer

We are looking for a senior infrastructure engineer to help scale our distributed
systems. You will design and operate high-throughput services, own reliability, and
improve developer productivity across the platform.

Responsibilities:
- Design and operate distributed systems handling millions of events per second
- Build and maintain event-driven pipelines with Kafka
- Own reliability, on-call, and incident response for core services
- Improve CI/CD and deployment tooling for engineering teams
- Mentor engineers and drive technical standards

Requirements:
- Strong experience with Python and Go
- Deep experience with Kafka, distributed systems, and microservices
- Hands-on with AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, and Docker
- Experience with observability: metrics, tracing, and structured logging
- Track record of improving reliability and developer experience at scale
